# Elysian Ledger — The Narrative Companion for Disco Elysium

**A non-intrusive, lore-aware utility for exploring the fractured psyche of Revachol without breaking the fourth wall.**

---

## 🤖 What Is Elysian Ledger?

Elysian Ledger is a **polished, fan-made companion tool** designed for players of *Disco Elysium* who want to **document, annotate, and revisit** their playthroughs with the precision of a forensic accountant and the soul of a poet. It is not a modification, not a memory editor, and not a shortcut—it is a **digital marginalia system** that lives beside your game, quietly cataloging every thought, every failed dice roll, and every cryptic dialogue tree.

Think of it as **a notebook that remembers what you forgot**—a co-pilot for your internal monologue. Where the game challenges you to piece together a shattered identity, Elysian Ledger helps you keep the fragments organized, searchable, and beautifully interlinked.

---

## 🧠 Core Philosophy

*Disco Elysium* is a game about **conversations with yourself**. Elysian Ledger extends that metaphor into real life. Instead of pausing to scribble notes on paper, you open a **minimal, keyboard-driven interface** that feels like an extension of your own cognition.

We built this tool because the game's narrative density—its political rants, its haunting side-quests, its absurdist humor—deserves a **bibliographic index** worthy of an archive. This is not a walkthrough. This is **your personal epistemological toolkit**.

## ✨ Feature Compendium

### 📖 The Thought Cabinet Synchronizer
Import your in-game "Thought Cabinet" selections manually or via a simple copy-paste protocol (no file alterations, no memory scraping). The Ledger then generates **interactive relationship maps** showing which thoughts conflict, which synergize, and which are pure self-destruction.

### 🗺️ Revachol Cartographer
Plot your exploratory journey on a **text-based map grid**. Mark locations, add atmospheric descriptions, and attach key dialogue excerpts. The Cartographer supports **layered annotations** for different playthroughs, so you can compare how your detective evolves across multiple realities.

### 🧾 Dialogue Transcript Archiver
Paste any conversation snippet from the game (or transcribe it yourself). The Ledger's **semantic tagging engine** automatically categorizes dialogue by emotional tone (e.g., "Despair," "Dry Wit," "Cosmic Horror"), political alignment, and associated skill checks. This enables powerful future searches like: *"show me all instances where I failed a Rhetoric check and felt ashamed."*

### 🔍 Semantic Search Engine
Forget keyword matching. Elysian Ledger uses **contextual similarity search** (via a local, offline processing model) to find related passages based on *meaning*. Ask it to "find moments of quiet hope" and it will surface the subtle sunrises, not just the word "hope."

### 🎨 The Aesthetic Mode
A warm, paper-like theme that mimics the game's oil-painting aesthetic. Choose between **"Le Caillou Rain"** (grey-blue), **"The Copper Cup"** (amber), and **"Pale Static"** (sterile white). Every theme is fully responsive, from a 4K monitor down to a smartphone screen.

### 🌐 Multilingual Margins
Full support for writing and reading your annotations in **English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, and Russian**. The interface itself translates, while your notes remain in your original language—or you can enable auto-translation for instant cross-referencing.

### ⏰ 24/7 Narrative Support (Community)
A dedicated **community forum portal** where players share thematic write-ups, lore theories, and personal interpretation frameworks. While this is not an official support channel, our community stewards are available around the clock to help you navigate the Ledger's deeper features or discuss the philosophical implications of the Pale.

The setup process is as seamless as **breathing in the damp air of Revachol**—you'll be cataloging your first thought before the opening credits finish rolling.

## 🛠️ Advanced Usage Scenarios

### The Archivist's Ritual
1. **Capture** – While playing, use the system clipboard to copy dialogue snippets.
2. **Paste & Tag** – Insert them into the Ledger; the tagging engine proposes classifications.
3. **Connect** – Link the snippet to a location, a thought, or a character.
4. **Reflect** – At the end of a session, use the "Ambient Review" mode to see a chronological, aesthetically formatted recap of your emotional journey.

### The Multi-Playthrough Scholar
Maintain separate "Timelines" for different runs (e.g., "High Intellect, Low Morale," "Apocalyptic Fascist," "Sorry Cop"). The Ledger's **differential compare** tool highlights what changed between your runs, including which dialogue options you never encountered.
